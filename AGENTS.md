# ISTRIADE Corporate Website — Agent Guardrails

This repository is the corporate hub for ISTRIADE GROUP LLC.

## Non-negotiable boundaries
- Do not publish, merge to `main`, change DNS, or alter production origin without explicit Founder authorization.
- Do not introduce product pricing, tiers, trials, discounts, checkout, or product-specific contractual terms into the corporate website.
- Do not reintroduce obsolete portfolio positioning: Amazon FBA, Amazon Seller, NSF, SGS, Shopify Growth Signals, Panelize, HEY-Commerce, or dropshipping.
- Do not redraw or modify canonical ISTRIADE Signal Vector assets. Brand masters under `public/brand/istriade/` must preserve their recorded hashes.
- Do not activate external product links until the product website is public and explicitly authorized.
- Corporate contact remains email-first at `contact@istriadegroup.com` unless governance changes.

## Lifecycle discipline
Proposal → approval → build → test → validation → deployment → commercial activation are distinct states. Never inflate status.
