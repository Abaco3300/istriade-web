## Current Crunchbase creation requirements

Crunchbase currently states that a user must be registered and socially authenticated before contributing profile data. It also instructs contributors to search for an existing profile before creating a new one.

For company profiles, Crunchbase recommends completing items including:

- company logo;
- founded date;
- website and social links;
- short and long company descriptions;
- headquarters;
- 3–5 industries;
- founders;
- funding rounds where applicable;
- employees, board members and advisors where applicable.

Crunchbase content guidance requires factual, objective descriptions in the third person and discourages marketing language. General company email addresses are preferred over personal email addresses.
